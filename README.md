### Iguana Music Player is a lightweight, minimalist music player designed to provide a smooth audio experience on Linux systems.

**Key features**:

- No installation and no dependencies: It runs on any Linux distribution as a single executable that you can carry on a USB drive and open on any computer.
- It offers a straightforward way to listen to your music without relying on internet connections or cluttered interfaces.
